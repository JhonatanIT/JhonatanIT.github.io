Delete your VibeWave account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open VibeWave and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out when it is done.

If you no longer have the app

Email jhonatan.ibatac@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my VibeWave account" and your Spotify display name. I will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile and taste card, friend code, shared activity, reactions, comments and friend connections (in both directions) are removed from the VibeWave server, and the data on your phone is cleared. Server request logs expire on their own within 30 days.

What is not touched

Your Spotify account and your playlists on Spotify. To remove VibeWave's access to Spotify, visit spotify.com/account/apps.
