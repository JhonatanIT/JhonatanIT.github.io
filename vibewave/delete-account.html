Delete your VibeWave account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open VibeWave and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out when it is done.

If you no longer have the app

Email jhonatan.pp2207@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my VibeWave account". Tell me how you signed in (Spotify or Google) and the name shown on your VibeWave profile, so I can find it. I will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

If you only used VibeWave as a guest, there is no profile on the server to delete. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything it kept on your phone.

What is deleted

Your profile and taste card, friend code, saved concerts, shared activity, reactions, comments and friend connections (in both directions) are removed from the VibeWave server, and the data on your phone is cleared. Server request logs expire on their own within 30 days.

What is not touched

Your Spotify account and your playlists on Spotify, and your Google account. To remove VibeWave's access to Spotify, visit spotify.com/account/apps. For Google, see myaccount.google.com/connections.
