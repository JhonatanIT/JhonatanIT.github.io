VibeWave privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

VibeWave is an Android app that suggests new music and concerts based on your Spotify listening, and lets you share what you are listening to with friends. It is made by Jhonatan Ibañez in Auckland, New Zealand ("I", "me"). You can reach me at jhonatan.ibatac@gmail.com.

VibeWave has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or rent your data.

What stays on your phone

Your sign-in tokens, a cache of your music and concert data, your saved concerts, and your friends and feed are stored in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backup. Logging out clears friends and feed from the phone.

Who else sees data

  • Spotify processes your sign-in and listening data under its own privacy policy. You can revoke VibeWave's access any time at spotify.com/account/apps.
  • Ticketmaster and Last.fm receive the searches described above, from the VibeWave server, not from your phone, so they do not see your IP address.
  • Microsoft Azure hosts the VibeWave server and database.
  • Your friends see your taste card and, if sharing is on, your shared activity.

How long it is kept

Server data is kept until you delete your account. Server request logs are kept for up to 30 days. Turning off "Share my activity" stops new sharing but does not remove what you already shared; deleting your account does.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: in the app, Profile, "Delete my account". This removes your profile, shared activity, reactions, comments and friend connections from the server and clears the phone. Details: how to delete your account.
  • See or correct your data: most of it is visible in the app (your profile and feed). Email me for anything else.
  • Location is optional and can be turned off in Android settings at any time.

Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 (and similar laws elsewhere) you can ask me for a copy of your personal information and ask me to correct it. If you are unhappy with how I handle it, you can complain to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Security

All traffic between the app, the server and the services above uses HTTPS. The server does not store any password: sign-in is done by Spotify.

Children

VibeWave is not meant for children under 13, and Spotify's own age limits apply.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, I will update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
